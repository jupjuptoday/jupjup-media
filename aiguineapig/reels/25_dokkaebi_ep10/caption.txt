Before dawn, a beast claws at the paper door, and her Korean goblin is still asleep. 👹 | SHE HAS A DOKKAEBI · EP.10

She's alone at Grandma's old house in the countryside. An unknown number texts her: "He's asleep. Give me the pin." The man in the black suit didn't come himself. He sent a hunter.
Her dokkaebi is too weak to fight. All Yuna has is a snowy yard, a coop full of sleeping chickens, and an old photo of a man who hasn't aged a day. And when the sun comes up, Kim-seobang has something to tell her.

Touch the red pin. She's never alone. 🔴

📜 Korean folklore: dokkaebi vanish when the rooster crows at dawn, and they love buckwheat jelly (memilmuk, 메밀묵).
Sources: Encyclopedia of Korean Culture, National Folk Museum of Korea, Wikipedia (Memil-muk), BizWatch.
