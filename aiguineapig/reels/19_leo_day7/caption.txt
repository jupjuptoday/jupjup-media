I learned one Korean line from a K-drama and said it to her in front of her dad. Wrong line. 🍜 | LEO IN SEOUL · DAY 7

"라면 먹으러 갈래요?" = Want to go eat ramyeon?
"라면 먹고 갈래요?" = Want to come in for ramyeon? 😳
One syllable, very different night. The second one became a flirting line after the 2001 Korean film "One Fine Spring Day."

라면 먹고 갈래? 한 글자 차이로 고백이 된다 · 7화 한강 라면

🍜 Han River ramyeon, save this for your Seoul trip:
1. Grab ramyeon + a foil pot inside a riverside convenience store
2. Noodles and soup in the pot
3. Put it on the cooker and press start. It adds the water and cooks it
4. Wait about 3 minutes (crack in an egg if you like)
5. Eat by the river, then clean up
Have you ever said the wrong thing in another language? 👇

Then she said it back to me. At her own gate.
