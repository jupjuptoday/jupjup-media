🥬 Kimjang (김장), save this for a Korean winter:
1. Late November to early December, families and neighbors make kimchi together for the whole winter
2. Salted napa cabbage, then red filling spread leaf by leaf
3. Fold it into jars and share with neighbors
4. The reward: boiled pork wrapped in fresh kimchi (bossam)
UNESCO listed kimjang as Intangible Cultural Heritage in 2013.
