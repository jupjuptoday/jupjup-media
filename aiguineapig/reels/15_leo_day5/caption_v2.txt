🌸 Tip: Bongsuna (봉숭아) is Korean balsam-flower nail dye. In summer, people crush the petals and leaves, wrap them on their nails overnight, and by winter only the tips are still orange. The saying goes: if the color lasts until the first snow, your first love comes true.
❄️ Seoul's first snow comes around Nov 20 on average (Korea Meteorological Administration, 1991-2020 normal).
